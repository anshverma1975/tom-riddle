Diary settings

Connect your oracle — any OpenAI-compatible API. Your key stays in this browser only. Works with OpenAI, OpenRouter, Groq, Gemini (via OpenAI compat), or a local server.

Tip: for quick testing leave Base empty to use OpenAI default. For OpenRouter use https://openrouter.ai/api/v1. Each page is sent as a small PNG to the model you configure — nothing else leaves the device.

How to use the diary

No buttons need to be pressed while writing — the pen is the only interface. Just write, then rest.

Write, then rest 2.8s

The diary drinks your ink and Tom replies. His answer writes itself stroke by stroke, then fades.

Erase

Tap Eraser and draw over ink to erase, or use your stylus eraser. Tap Pen to write again.

"Show me what I wrote about…"

Write a request like show me the page about the garden and the remembered page rises through the paper — your handwriting and Tom's old reply, in faded ink. Tap anywhere to return.

"What do you remember?"

Tom lists what he remembers.

Privacy

Memories live only in this browser's storage. Clear them anytime in Settings → Forget all memories. Turn memory off to send no history.

Remembered pages

Tap a page to conjure it. The diary keeps the last ~400 pages on this device.
